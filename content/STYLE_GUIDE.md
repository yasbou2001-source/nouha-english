# Nouha English — Style guide (READ FULLY before writing any content)

## Who is the learner
- Nouha: Moroccan adult woman. She speaks Moroccan Darija. She does NOT understand French, and Standard-Arabic grammar jargon confuses her. Beginner in English (A1 → A2).
- ALL explanations, instructions, translations and feedback are in **Moroccan Darija written in LATIN letters** (the "SMS / Facebook" style Moroccans use every day). NEVER use Arabic script anywhere. NEVER explain in French or English.
- Address Nouha directly, in the FEMININE: "nti", "kat3rfi", "diri", "9oli", "ktbi", "sem3i", "jawbi", "khtari", "9ray", "ratbi", "3awdi", "jarrbi", "chofi", "fekkri", "tkellmi", "wessli". Use her name sometimes ("Mzyan, Nouha!").

## Darija spelling conventions (be consistent!)
- 3 = ع  (3afak, 3lach, 3ref, 3la, m3a, 3ndi, 3am, 3a2ila)
- 7 = ح  (7na, 7aja, 7it, 7wayj, s7i7, mer7ba, ss7a, 7iwar)
- 9 = ق  (9ra, wa9t, b9a, 9bel, 9al, l9ism, sso9, 9ehwa)
- kh = خ (khass, khouya, khedma, khayb, lkhir) — never use "5"
- gh = غ (ghadi, ghir, bghit, ghedda, ghalet)
- ch = ش (chno, kifach, chwiya, choukran, chkoun, chhar)
- "ou" = the /u/ vowel (khouya, houma, ntouma, choufi, lyouma) ; "w" = the consonant and "and" (ana w nti)
- 2 = hamza, only when needed (so2al, mas2ala, li9a2)
- Attach the article to the noun: lktab, ddar, ssou9, lwa9t, lkhedma, lmeghrib, lingliziya, l3a2ila, lmdina. Apostrophe only when it really helps reading: l'oustad, l'ism.
- Use THESE exact forms: ana, nti, nta, howa, hiya, 7na, ntouma, houma / hada, hadi, hadou, had / dak, dik, douk / dyal, dyali, dyalek, dyalo, dyalha, dyalna, dyalhom / bzzaf, chwiya, daba, dima, lyouma, ghedda, lbare7, sbah, l3chiya, lil / wach, chno, fin, kifach, 3lach, chkoun, fo9ach, ch7al, achmen / mzyan, zwin, zwina, sahl, s3ib, khayb / bhal, ya3ni, yallah, safi, wakha, khass, khassek, bghit, 3ndi, 3ndek, kayn, kayna, kaynin, machi, walou, ghir, 7ta, 7it, walakin, ila, melli, 9bel, men ba3d, m3a, f, 3la, men, l, 3nd.
- Present tense: ka- prefix → kan9ra (I), kat9ray (you f.), kay9ra (he), kat9ra (she), kan9raw (we), kat9raw (you pl.), kay9raw (they). Future: "ghadi" + verb (ghadi n9ra / ghadi t9ray). Negation: ma…ch (makan3refch, makaymchich, machi).
- Borrowings that ARE everyday Darija are fine: tomobil, tilifoun, portable, kouzina, tabla, bisklita, lotil, lmatar, lgar, tobis, taxi, pc, lbanka, lfarmasyan (prefer "sseydaliya" + gloss), lbiro.
- Punctuation: normal; no space before ? and !.

## Pedagogy
- CEFR A1 → A2. Short sentences. One idea per paragraph. Concrete everyday Moroccan life (Casablanca, Rabat, Marrakech, Agadir, Fes, Tanger; ssou9, couscous, tagine, atay, dirham; family, work, market, café, bus, taxi, doctor, travel, phone).
- Explain English by COMPARING with Darija: "bhal f darija melli kan9oulo…". Grammar words only with a simple gloss, e.g. "l fi3l (verb) = lkelma li katbyen l7araka, bhal `eat`, `go`".
- Inside ANY Darija text (body_da, explain_da, hint_da, question_da, context_da, prompt_da, tip…), EVERY English word or phrase must be wrapped in backticks: `Hello`. The app turns them into clickable audio pills. Never put Darija inside backticks.
- "da" translations are natural Darija, not word-for-word.
- "pron" = approximate English pronunciation written with the SAME Darija-Latin conventions, syllables separated by hyphens. Vowel keys: ou=/u/, é=/eɪ/ or /e/, aï=/aɪ/, aw=/aʊ/, eu=/ɜː/ or /ə/, i=/ɪ/ or /iː/, a=/æ/ or /ɑː/, o=/ɒ/ or /oʊ/. Consonants: ch=/ʃ/, j=/ʒ/, dj=/dʒ/, "th" stays "th" (explain once: lsan bin ssnan), "r" = English r. Examples: Hello → "hé-lo", Nice to meet you → "naïs tou mit you", Where → "wér", water → "wo-teur", girl → "geurl", thank you → "thank you".
- English must be natural, correct, modern, correctly capitalized and punctuated. Use contractions in dialogues (I'm, What's, don't).
- Nouha is a woman: sample answers and role-plays are from her point of view ("I'm a student", "My husband/brother…" only if the prompt says so — keep her situation neutral: adult, Moroccan, learning English, lives in Morocco).
- Exercises: ONE unambiguous correct answer. Distractors plausible but clearly wrong. Mixed types, easy → harder. Only test vocabulary/grammar from this lesson or previous lessons (see SYLLABUS.md).
- `order` exercises: "words" must contain EXACTLY the words of "answer" (punctuation attached to the last word, e.g. "Nouha."). 4–8 words.
- `translate` exercises: give 2–4 accepted "answers" (full form + contraction, obvious synonyms).
- Role-play "expect_keywords": lowercase, short, several alternatives (any one found in what Nouha says = success). Include very likely phrasings.
- Reading text: A1.1–A1.2 → 60–110 words; A2 → 110–170 words. Give "text_da" (full Darija translation) and a glossary of 6–10 words.
- Listening dialogue: 6–10 lines, exactly 2 speakers with names (Sarah, Omar, Mr. Brown, Amina, Tom, Laila, Waiter, Doctor…). "pre_da" tells her what to listen for.
- Variety matters: avoid repeating the same exercise pattern; rotate mcq / listen_mcq / fill / order / translate / dictation / match / truefalse / speak. Each lesson must contain at least: 2 listen_mcq, 1 dictation, 1 match, 2 order, 2 translate, 1 speak, 2 fill, 2 mcq.
